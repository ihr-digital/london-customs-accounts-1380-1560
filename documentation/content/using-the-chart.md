# The Chart

**[Open the Chart](https://ihr-digital.github.io/london-customs-accounts-1380-1560/?view=chart)** — opens in the site itself.

The Chart plots the ladings **currently passing the Table's filters** against
the enrolled customs totals, so it answers "how much of the recorded trade does
this filtered set account for?"

## Controls

- **Accounting year start** — sets the date the accounting year begins on.
  Medieval customs years do not start in January, and the choice moves every
  boundary in the series.
- **Aggregation** — monthly, quarterly or yearly.
- **Reset Zoom** — returns to the full time axis.

## Zooming

- Mouse wheel, or click and drag a selection.
- Pinch on a touch device.

## The enrolled totals

- The comparison series is the **Enrolled Accounts**: the crown's own annual
  totals, against which the surviving particular accounts are a sample.
- Sources are listed behind *various sources* beneath the chart, compiled by
  Maria Grove-Gordillo.
- The Excel icon downloads that compilation.

:::{note}
The chart follows the Table's filters. A narrow filter will show a small series
against a large enrolled total — that gap is the point of the comparison, not a
fault in it.
:::
